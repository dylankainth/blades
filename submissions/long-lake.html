What we refused to build

The twins never decide.
They do the awkward part.

Filtering a thousand strangers, saying the honest thing, admitting when there is no real reason to meet — the invisible labour people avoid. Then they hand the connection back to a human and get out of the way.

“Your twins found a reason — here it is, want to say hi?” beats “your twins already introduced you.”

KlickThe design rule
Proof, not promise

Every guardrail is
something you can watch.

A reason, never a score
The output is one plain sentence about why two people should talk. No percentage, no “87% match” — the reasoning is the product.
Dismissals stay private
When twins decide there is no real reason to meet, the names, reasoning and transcript are never published — not even to judges. Open the dashboard and try.
Both sides approve before names appear
Identity is anonymised until two people have each said yes. A twin cannot expose you to someone you did not agree to meet.
“Say hi” is a human tap
Nothing is ever sent on your behalf. No auto-generated intro, no auto-booked meeting, no message you did not write.
